What does out-of-band management mean?

On a suitably configured x86 platform, a specialist can monitor, restart or recover a deployed player even when its main operating system is unavailable. The exact OOB, security and recovery stack is selected and validated for each customer program.

Display technology, made visible

Move the controls to see what each technology contributes to the viewing experience.

Standard backlightLocal dimming
01

Light only where it’s needed.

Local dimming divides the backlight into independently controlled zones. Dark areas can stay dark while highlights remain bright.

Deeper blacksImproved perceived contrastBetter highlightsBright detail without washing out shadowsPremium impactMore dimensional, lifelike images
STANDARDQUANTUM DOT
02

Purity creates colour.

Quantum dots convert blue backlight energy into precise red and green wavelengths, producing a wider and more accurate colour range.

Wider gamutRicher reds and greensHigh brightnessStrong colour at demanding luminanceColour accuracyMore controlled spectral output
03

Colour—with direction.

Quantum rods are elongated nanocrystals. When aligned, they can emit directionally polarised light—opening paths to more efficient display architectures.

Polarised outputPotentially less light lost through polarisationHigh colour purityNarrow spectral emissionDesign potentialEfficiency and optical-stack opportunities
Quantum-rod availability and performance are application- and supply-chain dependent; final integration requires engineering confirmation.
